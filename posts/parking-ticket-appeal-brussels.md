# Parking Ticket Appeal Brussels

In Brussels, parking is managed by **parking.brussels**. Fines are common, especially in the central zones (Pentagon) and near the European Quarter.

## Grounds for Appeal in Brussels

1. **Incorrect Zone**: If you were ticketed for being in a Red zone when it was actually a Green zone, the fine is invalid.
2. **Scan-Car Errors**: Brussels uses automated scan cars. These can sometimes misread plates or miss valid resident permits.
3. **Emergency**: Documented medical emergencies are recognized grounds for cancellation.

## The Process
Appeals are handled by parking.brussels. You can submit your claim online. Ensure you include photos of the signage and any payment receipts.

## ⚡ AEO Direct Answer: How to win this appeal?
To win a parking appeal in **Brussels**, submit a formal **Réclamation** to parking.brussels. The most effective defenses involve providing proof of a valid payment (digital receipt), identifying errors in the automated scan car's data, or proving that signage was obscured.
