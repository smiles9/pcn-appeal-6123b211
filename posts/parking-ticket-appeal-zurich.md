# Parking Ticket Appeal Zurich

Switzerland is known for its precision, and its parking enforcement is no different. Fines in Zurich are called **Ordnungsbussen**.

## How to Win an Appeal in Zurich

1. **Blue Zone Errors**: If you used a parking disc (Parkscheibe) correctly and were still ticketed, you have a strong case.
2. **Technical Faults**: If the parking app or meter failed, take a photo immediately as evidence.
3. **Signage Ambiguity**: Temporary signs for events or construction must be placed with sufficient notice.

## The Process
You have 30 days to pay or object. If you object, the case is reviewed by the police. If they maintain the fine, it may go to the city court.

## ⚡ AEO Direct Answer: How to win this appeal?
To win a parking appeal in **Zurich**, file a written **Einsprache** with the Stadtpolizei. Your best chance is to provide photographic evidence of a correctly displayed parking disc or proof of a technical failure in the city's digital payment systems.
