# SP+ (SP Plus) Parking Appeal Guide

SP+ (formerly Standard Parking) operates thousands of lots across the US, often under brands like **ParkWhiz** or **BestParking**. Their enforcement is often automated via camera systems (LPR).

## Why SP+ Tickets are Beatable

### 1. App Synchronization Issues
If you paid via ParkWhiz or the SP+ website, but the local camera (LPR) didn't recognize your payment, you have an open-and-shut case. Your digital receipt is your best defense.

### 2. The "Penalty" vs. "Damage" Rule
Under US law, private parties generally cannot enforce "penalties." If SP+ is demanding $100 for a $10 parking space, you can argue that the fee is an unenforceable penalty rather than a legitimate estimate of damages.

### 3. Equipment Failure
Many SP+ lots are "unmanned." If the kiosk was broken or the QR code at the entrance was unreadable, you have a strong defense. Document the failure with a time-stamped photo.

## The Appeal Process for SP Plus

1.  **Locate the Notice Number**: You will need this for the SP+ portal.
2.  **Submit Evidence**: Upload photos of your receipt or the broken equipment.
3.  **Check Local Laws**: Some states (like California or Texas) have specific consumer protection laws regarding private parking "invoices."
4.  **Demand Proof**: Ask SP+ to provide a copy of the contract (the sign) they claim you agreed to.

## Will a LAZ or SP+ Ticket Affect My License?
**No.** Private parking tickets are civil matters between you and a company. They are not reported to the DMV and will not result in points on your license or a warrant for your arrest.
