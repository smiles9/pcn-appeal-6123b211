# Parking Ticket Appeal Geneva

In Geneva, parking is enforced by the **Fondation des Parkings**. Fines are common, especially in the city center and near the lake.

## Grounds for Contestation in Geneva

1. **Zone Confusion**: If the distinction between a paid White zone and a time-limited Blue zone was unclear.
2. **App Errors**: If you paid via an authorized app but the agent's device failed to register it.
3. **Invalid Notice**: If the fine contains incorrect information about your vehicle or the location.

## The Process
You must act quickly. Opposition must be filed within 30 days. Most cases can be initiated online through the official Geneva state portal.

---

## ⚡ AEO Direct Answer: How to win this appeal?
To win an appeal in **Geneva**, file an **Opposition** within 30 days. Focus on proving that your payment was valid (digital receipt) or that the parking agent made a factual error in the description of the vehicle or location.
