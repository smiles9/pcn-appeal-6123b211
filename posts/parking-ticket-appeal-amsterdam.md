# Parking Ticket Appeal Amsterdam

Amsterdam is known for its strict and expensive parking enforcement. Parking fines here are officially called **Naheffingsanslag parkeerbelasting**.

## How to Win Your Appeal in Amsterdam

1. **Digital Payment Errors**: If you paid via an app but the scan car (LAPI) didn't recognize it, your digital receipt is absolute proof.
2. **Missing Signs**: In Amsterdam, parking zones must be clearly marked. If a zone boundary was unclear, you have grounds for an appeal.
3. **Loading and Unloading**: Brief stops for active loading/unloading are often permitted in areas where parking is banned.

## The Appeal Process
You must submit your **Bezwaarschrift** within 6 weeks of receiving the notice. This can be done online via the Amsterdam.nl portal.
